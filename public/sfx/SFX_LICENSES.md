## 4. Acoustic Calibration & Polyphony Guidelines for Remotion AudioMixer

### 4.1 Headroom Budgeting
When multiple sound cues overlap (such as `transition-whoosh-heavy` occurring over `ambient-beat` background track with a concurrent `click-switch`), the linear sum of amplitudes may exceed 1.0 (0 dBFS), causing digital clipping in AAC/MP4 encoders:

$$\sum_{i=1}^N A_i > 1.0 \implies \text{Digital Distortion / Clipping}$$

To preserve pristine audio fidelity:
1. **Background Track Staging**:
   - Master volume for `ambient-beat.wav` is calibrated to $0.32 \pm 0.03$ (-9.9 dB relative attenuation).
   - In-composition fade-in: 15 frames (0.5s).
   - In-composition fade-out: 30 frames (1.0s).
2. **Transient SFX Staging & Ducking Priority**:
   - **Priority 2 (Triggers -5.2 dB sidechain ducking of music)**:
     - `camera-whoosh.wav`: volume $0.65$
     - `transition-whoosh-heavy.wav`: volume $0.70$
     - `transition-whoosh-fast.wav`: volume $0.65$
     - `sound-riser.wav`: volume $0.75$
     - `sub-bass-drop.wav`: volume $0.85$
     - `chime.wav`: volume $0.75$
     - `success-chime.wav`: volume $0.80$
   - **Priority 1 (Subtle micro-interactions, no music ducking)**:
     - `mouse-click.wav`: volume $0.85$
     - `click-switch.wav`: volume $0.75$
     - `click-soft.wav`: volume $0.60$
     - `keyboard-tap.wav`: volume $0.50$
     - `mechanical-tap-1..4.wav`: volume $0.50$ (round-robin alternating)
     - `soft-pop.wav`: volume $0.65$
     - `pop-clean.wav`: volume $0.70$
3. **Master C^1 Soft-Knee Limiter**:
   - The `AudioMixer` module applies soft-knee limiting ($L(S) \le 0.95$ when $S > 0.75$) to guarantee absolute zero digital clipping under high polyphony.
